HOW TO USE

(go back to content list)

Before First Use

  • Place the product on a flat and stable surface.
  • Remove any protective materials.

Basic Operations

Rotate the product to start the timer.

  • Rotate the lamp 180 degrees.
  • The timer starts automatically.
  • The light gradually changes as time passes.

When the timer ends, the light turns off automatically.

Light Behavior

The light intensity changes to indicate remaining time.

  • Bright light: Timer has just started.
  • Dim light: Timer is nearing completion.
  • Light off: Timer has ended.

Maintenance

Clean the product regularly to maintain performance.

  • Turn off the product before cleaning.
  • Use a soft, dry cloth.
  • Do not use water or cleaning agents.

Do not attempt to repair the product yourself.

Troubleshooting

  • If the timer does not start, the product may not be rotated correctly. Rotate the product 180 degrees to start the timer.
  • If the light does not change, the timer may not be activated. Restart the timer and check the product operation.
